01 / THE IDEA

A long stream.
A memory for the small details.

Understanding a live video means connecting what is happening now with what happened before.

MEMO builds multi-level, entity-aware structured memory as the video arrives. It uses lightweight indices to find relevant moments, while keeping high-resolution visual evidence available for reasoning.

01

Follow scenes and entities

Global semantics, local entity features, and spatial structure jointly track continuity.

02

Keep the evidence accessible

GPU-resident retrieval indices point to high-resolution evidence stored on CPU.

03

Recall for the question

Relevant past chunks join the current segment to support MLLM reasoning.
